# The Slasher's Ejection immunity plays out as a tied vote, not an excluded ballot option

Participants can still vote for The Slasher during a Meeting — they're not removed from the ballot, and they can even "win" the tally outright with a real majority. The Ejection just never happens: it resolves exactly like a tied vote always has, with "No one was ejected."

The alternative was excluding The Slasher from the ballot entirely, so nobody could vote for them in the first place. That would have been clearer (nobody wastes a vote wondering why nothing happened) but also flatly announces the immunity to the whole table the first time someone tries to add them to the ballot and can't. Resolving as a tie instead means the group can't immediately tell *why* the vote failed — a wasted vote and a failed assassination attempt look identical from the outside, which fits the character a lot better than a UI-level "this option is unavailable."
