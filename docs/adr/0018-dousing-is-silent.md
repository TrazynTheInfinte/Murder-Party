# Dousing is invisible to the victim

Being Doused by the Arsonist produces no message, sound, or other signal to the victim. The only participant who ever sees dousing progress is the Arsonist themselves.

This is the opposite choice from the Noisemaker, whose entire point is a loud, public death alert — and it would have been easy to assume "being targeted by a Neutral" should always surface some kind of warning, the way it does everywhere else a Participant's fate is on the line (Weapon hits, Ejections, Noisemaker deaths). Dousing stays silent on purpose: the Arsonist's whole identity is "the group doesn't realize everyone's already marked until it's too late," and a visible tell would let the group simply gang up on whoever's doing the marking well before they can finish.
