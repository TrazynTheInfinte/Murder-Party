# Reporting a Corpse does not spend the Reporter's Meeting Call

Every other way to trigger a Meeting — the Panic Button, the Captain's horn — spends the triggering Participant's one-per-Round Meeting Call. Reporting is the exception: looting a Corpse's Report Item always triggers a Meeting, even if the Reporter already used their Call earlier in the Round.

This is deliberate, not an oversight, because the two mechanisms gate different things. A Meeting Call limits how often a Participant can interrupt free play on a hunch. Finding a Corpse is not a hunch — it is hard evidence a Strike happened, and it is already rate-limited by the Corpse's own lifespan and the fact that Reporting instantly destroys every other live Corpse (via the Meeting start logic). Spending a Call on it would mean a Participant who already (even wrongly) used their Call could stumble onto a body and have no way to report it, which defeats the point of Corpses carrying information at all.
