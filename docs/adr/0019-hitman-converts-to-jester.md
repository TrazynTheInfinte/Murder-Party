# A Hitman whose target dies first converts into a Jester

If the Hitman's assigned target is eliminated by anything other than the Hitman's own knife — the Killer strikes them, they're Ejected, whatever — the Hitman doesn't just lose their shot and sit out the rest of the Round as a bystander. They convert into a Jester on the spot: their target-locked knife is replaced with the Jester's decoy knife, and their new Personal Win becomes getting themselves Ejected.

This was a deliberate design call, not the default "their objective is now unreachable, they're done" outcome a future reader would expect. It keeps every Neutral Round meaningfully playable to the end regardless of how the Hitman's target dies, instead of occasionally reducing one Participant to a do-nothing spectator-in-waiting for the remainder of the Round.
