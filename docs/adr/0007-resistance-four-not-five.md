# Participants get Resistance IV, not full (V) damage immunity

Every Participant gets Resistance IV plus a full heal every tick while a Round is active, rather than Resistance V or the `Invulnerable` entity flag, even though the goal is that nothing but a Strike can ever actually eliminate someone. Full immunity risks silently breaking the Weapon's hit detection, which relies on an advancement that fires off a real damage event (`minecraft:player_hurt_entity`) — if a hit is reduced to exactly zero damage, it's untested whether that event still fires at all. Resistance IV deliberately leaves a sliver of non-zero damage through, and the per-tick heal erases it before it can accumulate into anything lethal. Don't "fix" this to Resistance V without first confirming a fully-blocked hit still triggers the advancement.
