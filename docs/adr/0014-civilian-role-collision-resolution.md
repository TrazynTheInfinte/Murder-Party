# Civilian Role collisions resolve by reassigning from the remaining pool

Each Innocent's Civilian Role Choice is a random pair drawn independently, so two different Innocents can end up offered — and picking — the same Role. Rather than a simpler uniform-random assignment across all Innocents (which would need no collision handling at all, but loses the "your own private pair of options" feel), the first click on a Role locks it in; anyone who picks a Role that's already taken gets reassigned a random Role from whichever are still unclaimed at that moment, or stays a normal Innocent if none are left. This guarantees at most one Participant per Role per Round without needing a second round of possible collisions from the reassignment itself, since it only ever draws from what's provably still available.
